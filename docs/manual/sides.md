# The two sides

Blitter always shows two sides next to each other. One of them is the **active side**: the
**source**. The other one is the **destination**.

- Copy, move and compare work from the source to the destination: **F5** copies the active
  side's selection into the other side's folder, **F6** moves it there.
- Menu commands and shortcuts act on the active side.
- The active side's path field is drawn in the accent color.

## Changing the active side

- **Tab** switches to the other side (View → Switch Active Side).
- Clicking anywhere in a side makes it active: its list, its tree, its path field or its
  buttons.

## Sizes

- The splitter between the two sides, and the one between each side's tree and list, can be
  dragged. Blitter keeps their positions.
- Widening the window gives the extra room to the file lists. A tree keeps its width.
